Farwatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Farwatch: Logic Puzzle ("Farwatch") is a puzzle game made by Jeremy Slaton. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information kept on your device

Your display name, level, streaks, shields, awards, settings, unlocked styles and scenes, and any puzzle in progress are saved on your device only. We do not run a server and do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Daily challenge

The daily challenge standings are generated on your device. Other entries on the standings are computer players and are labeled as bots. Your name and scores are not sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app usage and device details. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in the game under Profile, then "Privacy and ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Update checks

When the game starts, it may read a small file from our website to check whether a newer version is required. This request does not include any personal information, but like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Farwatch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
