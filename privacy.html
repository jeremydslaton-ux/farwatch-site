Farwatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Farwatch: Logic Puzzle ("Farwatch") is a puzzle game made by Slaton Games. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Your game progress

Your display name, level, streaks, shields, awards, settings, unlocked styles and scenes, and any puzzle in progress are saved on your device.

When you are online, the game also keeps a backup copy of this progress in our cloud database (Google Firebase), so it is not lost. The game signs in anonymously: it creates a random player ID and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or Google account. Only your own copy of the game can read your backup.

Moving to a new phone

If you choose "Get a transfer code" in Profile, a copy of your progress is stored under a random code for up to 7 days. Anyone who enters that code can load the progress, so only share it with yourself. You can ignore the code and it expires on its own.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, we receive what you type and the face you choose, along with your random player ID, the game version, your level, and basic device information (the device and browser description your phone reports). We use it only to fix problems and improve the game. Please do not include personal information in feedback.

Usage statistics

In the Android app, the game sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as charts started and finished, board size and difficulty, hints used, and daily challenge results, along with standard device and app information. This tells us which features are used and where the game is too hard or too easy. It does not include your name or contact details.

The game also checks a small settings file from Firebase Remote Config, so we can adjust things like how often ads appear without releasing a new version.

Daily challenge

The daily challenge standings are currently generated on your device. Other entries on the standings are computer players and are labeled as bots.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app usage and device details. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in the game under Profile, then "Privacy and ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Update checks

When the game starts, it may read a small file from our website to check whether a newer version is required. This request does not include any personal information, but like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Farwatch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything stored on your device. To delete your cloud backup and feedback as well, email us at the address below and include the player ID shown in the game under Profile, About and we will delete it within 30 days. Backups that have not been used for 2 years may be deleted automatically.

Google Firebase processes this data for us. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
