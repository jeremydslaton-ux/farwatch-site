Delete your Farwatch data

Farwatch: Logic Puzzle, by Slaton Games

How to request deletion

  1. Open Farwatch and go to Profile. Scroll to About and note your player ID (shown as "player" followed by a short code).
  2. Email slatongames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Farwatch data" and include your player ID.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you no longer have the game installed and don't know your player ID, email us anyway with roughly when you last played and your display name, and we will do our best to find and delete your data.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud backup of game progress (display name, level, streaks, awards, unlocked styles and scenes, settings).
  • Any transfer codes you created.
  • Any feedback you sent from the game.
  • The anonymous sign-in record linked to your player ID.

What is kept

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are not linked to your name or contact details and are kept by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Data on your phone

Progress saved on your phone is deleted when you uninstall Farwatch or clear its data in Android settings.

See also the Farwatch Privacy Policy.
